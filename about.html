Our story

Built on honesty and quality

David started the business in March 1987. Nearly four decades later the sign on Caldarra Ave has had a fresh coat of paint, but the approach hasn’t changed: explain the problem in plain English, fix it properly, and charge a fair price.

Customers regularly tell us we found the faults others couldn’t, or saved them hundreds compared with the dealer. That’s why plenty of locals have been bringing their cars to us for years.

Timeline

1987 → today

A few milestones from almost four decades in Engadine.

1987

The business is born

David opens David’s Auto Electrics in March 1987 — the start of almost four decades of local auto electrical work.

2001

Industry finalist

Then based in Kirrawee, the business is named a finalist in the MTA & Daily Telegraph Motor Industry Awards — Auto Electricians division.

2002

Finalist again

Back among the state’s best as a finalist at the 2002 Motor Industry Awards, held at Star City in Sydney.

2006

Local award winner

Wins the Engadine Chamber of Commerce Small Business Awards in the Automotive category.

2023

36 years and counting

The team celebrates the business’s 36th birthday with locals who’ve stuck with them for decades.

Today

Still family, still local

David, his son Matt and Michelle run the Caldarra Ave workshop — now with free pick-up and delivery across the Shire.
